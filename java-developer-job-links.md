# Java Developer Job Application Links (India + Remote)

Where to apply for Java / Spring Boot / full-stack (Java + Angular) roles: fresher hiring programs, company careers pages, job boards, hiring tests, and government tech jobs.

**Last checked:** 11 October 2026. Every link was tested on that date (see [Link check](#link-check)).

---

## Read this first

- **Fresher drives only take certain graduation years.** Most mass-recruiter drives in 2026 are for the 2026 batch. Some (Infosys, Wipro) have also taken 2024/2025 graduates. Check the batch year and the "experience allowed" rule on each notice before you apply.
- **Fresher pay vs. experienced pay.** Prep sites report standard fresher tracks at roughly ₹3.5–6.5 LPA and higher tracks at roughly ₹6.5–9 LPA. Infosys Specialist Programmer (₹10–21 LPA) is the main exception. If you have under 2 years of experience, also check the same companies' regular **0–2 years** openings, which can pay more than their fresher tracks.
- **Six-month cool-off.** Infosys won't consider you again within 6 months of a previous Infosys interview. Check each company's notice for similar rules.
- **Never pay to apply.** Wipro, HCLTech, Accenture, LTM and others say they never charge fees. Apply only on official domains. Third-party "drive alert" sites are fine for news, but always apply on the company's own site.
